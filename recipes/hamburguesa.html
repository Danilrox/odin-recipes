Hamburguer

Description

image size: 612x503

A classic hamburger is made with a seasoned beef patty, a soft bun, cheese, lettuce, tomato, and onion. The beef patty is cooked in a hot pan or grill until browned and fully cooked, then topped with cheese and placed inside the toasted bun with the vegetables and your favorite sauces.

Ingredients

  • 300g ground beef
  • 2 hamburger buns
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 1 tomato
  • lettuce leaves
  • 1/2 onion
  • Salt an paper
  • Ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise
  • A little oil

Steps

  1. Divide the ground beef into two portions and shape them into patties.
  2. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a pan or grill with a little oil.
  4. Cook the patties for about 4–5 minutes on each side, until fully cooked.
  5. Add a slice of cheese to each patty and let it melt.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns lightly.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and your favorite sauce to the buns.
  8. Place the cooked patty inside the bun and serve hot.
