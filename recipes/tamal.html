Tamal

Description

First, the corn dough is prepared and seasoned with salt and spices. Then, the filling is made with meat, chicken, vegetables, and other ingredients. A portion of dough is placed on a banana leaf, and the filling is added in the center. The leaf is carefully folded and tied to keep the tamal closed. Finally, the tamales are steamed for several hours until the dough is fully cooked. They are served hot and can be enjoyed with coffee or other traditional drinks.

Ingredients

  • Corn dough
  • pork
  • carrots
  • Peas
  • Rice
  • onion
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Watter
  • Oil
  • Eggs

Steps

  1. Prepare the corn dough with water, salt, oil, and spices.
  2. Cook the chicken or pork and cut it into small pieces.
  3. Prepare the vegetables, such as carrots, peas, and onion.
  4. Wash and soften the banana leaves.
  5. Place a portion of corn dough on a banana leaf.
  6. Add the meat, vegetables, and other fillings in the center.
  7. Fold the banana leaf carefully and tie it with a string.
  8. Place the tamales in a steamer.
  9. Steam the tamales for about 1–2 hours, until the dough is fully cooked.
  10. Remove them from the steamer and serve them hot.
