Salchipapa

Description

A salchipapa is a popular Latin American fast food dish made with French fries and fried sausages. It is usually served with sauces such as ketchup, mayonnaise, and mustard, and sometimes topped with cheese, eggs, or other ingredients.

Ingredients

  • French fries
  • Sausages
  • Vegetable oil
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise
  • Mustard
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Wash and cut the potatoes into strips.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a pan or fryer.
  3. Fry the potatoes until they are golden and crispy.
  4. Cut the sausages into small pieces.
  5. Fry the sausages for a few minutes.
  6. Place the French fries on a plate.
  7. Add the fried sausages on top.
  8. Add ketchup, mayonnaise, and mustard.
  9. Add cheese or a fried egg if desired.
  10. Serve hot and enjoy!
